## Changed
- Loading bars now match Fortnite Porting: rounded track and fill with the card shadow, and the Home bar glides to each step over 5 seconds
- Start-up progress follows Fortnite Porting's weighted stages instead of a made-up estimate, so the bar jumps to the right place as each step begins
- The Files loader matches Fortnite Porting: glowing background, title, percentage and a 400 px bar
- When the progress is unknown (updating the file list) a spinning ring and the status show instead of a sliding bar
